## What tonight established (all merged or in PR #27)

**The night measurement channel works.** The scenery RTSP stream is blind after
dark — measured, not assumed: its ISP pins ~30 ms / gain 112.5 whatever you ask,
and sky vs terrain differ by 0.02/255. Star mode (`start_view("star")`) kills
RTSP but serves raw16 frames on the imaging channel (port 4800);
`client.capture_raw16_median()` reads it. 2 s exposures separate Bortle-8
skyglow from terrain at >100:1. Median per frame, never mean (M-12).

**The night detector works, validated against held-out ground truth.**
`sweep.night_find_edge`: the edge is the first *persistent, unrecovered* drop
against the running max; only negative steps are roughness. Six real profiles
from tonight are test fixtures (`tests/data/night_profiles_2026_08_07.json`),
each checked against the independent 2026-08-03 evening sweep — including a
canopy column correctly blocked and a glare column honestly refused.

**Checkpoint/resume works (terminus-58).** Every `orient` attempt appends one
line to `<out>_fiducials.jsonl` *as it completes*, with conditions. On resume,
cached columns are served without re-observation, and saved night profiles are
**re-judged by the current detector** — the night this was built, that turned
two wrong verdicts into right ones for free. Resumed coarse-only edges carry
`coarse_only: true`.

**`terminus orient` auto-selects the channel by Sun altitude** (< −12° → night).
This made two tests time-dependent until their Suns were pinned — any new test
touching `cmd_orient`/`_scope_measure` must pin `Sky.sun` (terminus-63, E-04).

## The frame caveat that matters for ANY cross-night work

Tonight's mount frame was **2.1° rotated** (wedge bumped during the day; Devon
plate solved and re-aligned). Consequences:

- Tonight's night fiducials (`captures/2026-08-07-night-proof/fiducials.jsonl`)
  are internally consistent but in the bumped frame. **Re-measure a few columns
  in the aligned frame before trusting them in a fit.**
- At steep horizon sections, 2.1° of azimuth ≈ 12°+ of apparent altitude (the
  tree edge runs ~6°alt/°az). An expectation check comparing az X to a
  reference at az X±5 **needs gradient-aware tolerance** or it manufactures
  disagreements exactly where the horizon is most informative. Not yet
  implemented anywhere — worth a ticket if you touch it.

## How to run things

```bash
# full photo pipeline, no telescope (documented in README "Reproduce it without a telescope")

terminus skymask pano.png --coverage pano.coverage.npy --classes pano.classes.npy --out photo_mask.yaml

    --fiducials captures/2026-08-03-evening/horizon_west.yaml --out oriented.yaml
terminus polar oriented.yaml   # one self-contained HTML page

# with the scope, day or night — channel auto-selected; checkpoints beside --out

# killed mid-run? just run it again: columns resume from <out>_fiducials.jsonl
```
